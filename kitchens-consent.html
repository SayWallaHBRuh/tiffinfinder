Listing consent form

Draft — a lawyer should review this wording before it's used with the first real kitchen.

A plain-language record that you're OK being listed on Tiffin Finder, with the details you've given us. Read it, ask any questions, then sign at the bottom — or just text or email the same words if that's easier. Back to For kitchens

What will show publicly

  • Your kitchen's name, cuisine, and diet words that apply (vegetarian-only, halal, Jain).
  • This week's menu, and your day, week and/or month prices.
  • Whether you offer a trial week, and whether you're taking new customers.
  • Your pickup spot, shown only at the precision you choose below — or your delivery neighbourhoods, or both.
  • The WhatsApp number or phone you want shown, so households can message or call you directly.
  • A "Permit checked" badge with the date we checked it, once your AHS Food Handling Permit is confirmed.

What will never show

  • Your home address, unless you specifically choose "Exact" pickup precision below and that address is the pickup spot you want shown.
  • Any personal contact other than the number you give us for your listing.
  • Anything about how you found out about Tiffin Finder or our conversation with you.
  • A claim that Alberta Health Services "approved" or "verified" you — a permit check is not an inspection, a grade, or an endorsement, and we say so on your page.

How pickup precision works

If you offer pickup, choose one. All three are normal, equally supported choices:

  • Exact

    Your real address. Shows "Pickup at …" plus a directions link.

  • Nearest intersection

    Like "36 St & 88 Ave NE". Shows "Pickup near …" plus a directions link.

  • Neighbourhood only

    Just your community name, like "Saddle Ridge". No address, no directions link. Most home kitchens choose this.

Changing your mind, later

You can ask us to change or remove your listing at any time — a menu update, a new price, a different pickup precision, a pause, or taking it down completely. Just email sitesbyadeel@gmail.com; our target is to respond and act within 48 hours of hearing from you. Nothing about this consent is shown on the site — it's kept only so we can show we had your permission.

Privacy (Alberta's PIPA)

  • Purpose. We collect what you give us only to build and run your public listing.
  • What's public vs. private. See "What will show publicly" and "What will never show" above.
  • Storage. Your listing details live in the site's public data file (the same way every listing does). This consent record itself — the fact you agreed, and when — is kept privately by Adeel, off the public site.
  • Retention. Kept for as long as your listing is live, plus a record that consent was given even after a listing comes down, in case it's ever needed to show permission was granted.
  • Contact. Questions about your data, any time: sitesbyadeel@gmail.com.

Free, no commission

Your listing, your weekly menu and your follower list are free, with no commission on anything you sell — see what's free, and what "Pro" will be on the For kitchens page.

Your agreement

"Yes, you can list [kitchen name] on Tiffin Finder with the information I've given you, shown the way described above."

Prefer to send this by text or email instead of signing on paper? Just send the sentence above in your own words to sitesbyadeel@gmail.com — that counts too.
