For kitchens

List your kitchen. Free, verified, yours.

You already cook for households across your quadrant. Tiffin Finder puts your weekly menu in front of the people looking for exactly that, with a permit-verified badge that tells them you’ve done things properly.

What a listing includes

A permit-verified badge

Households see “Permit verified” with the date we checked. It’s the first thing they look for and the reason they’ll pick you over a stranger on a marketplace post.

Your menu of the week

Five days, five dishes, your prices. Post it once and it shows on your page with day-by-day pricing and your delivery areas.

Followers who get told

People follow kitchens they like. When alerts launch, every follower hears the moment you post — no group chats to maintain.

Orders the way you already take them

Your page has one big “Order on WhatsApp” button and a “Call” button. Orders and payments stay between you and your customers. We never touch them and never take a cut.

Households can also browse a neighbourhood map. Your pin sits on a neighbourhood you deliver to, never on your address.

What’s free, and what “Pro” will be

Free, always: your verified listing, your weekly menu, your follower list and the alerts that go with it. There is no commission and no fee to be listed.

Pro, later: we plan optional paid extras for kitchens that want them — things like a featured spot in your quadrant, scheduling menus ahead, and simple stats on views and follows. None of this exists yet, there is no price yet, and nothing free will move behind Pro. We’ll tell listed kitchens first.

What verification means

Before we list you, we ask to see your current AHS Food Handling Permit and confirm it names the kitchen you actually cook in. When that checks out, your page shows “Permit verified” with the date we saw it.

Verification is our check that a valid permit exists. It is not an inspection, a grade, or an endorsement by Alberta Health Services, and we say so on every page. You agree to keep the permit valid, to tell us if anything changes, and to accept that we remove listings that receive an AHS closure order. The full wording is in the terms.

How to get listed

  1. 1

    Hold a valid permit

    If you don’t have one yet, start with how permits work. We can only list permitted kitchens — no exceptions, because the badge has to mean something.

  2. 2

    Send us your details

    Your kitchen’s name, quadrant, cuisine, the areas you deliver to, your WhatsApp number, and a photo or copy of your permit. Use the contact form; we’ll reply within a few days.

  3. 3

    We verify and publish

    We check the permit, build your page, and send you the link to approve before it goes live. You only appear with your permission.

  4. 4

    Post your weekly menu

    Send us the week’s five dishes and prices and we post them. Self-serve posting comes with a later version.

Preview note. The kitchens on the site right now are samples with made-up names and numbers. Real kitchens start being listed once verification is running, with their permission.
