For kitchens

List your kitchen. Free, permit-checked, yours.

You already cook for households across your quadrant. Tiffin Finder puts your weekly menu in front of the people looking for exactly that, with a permit-checked badge that tells them you’ve done things properly.

Not permitted yet? See how to get permitted

What a listing includes

A permit-checked badge

Households see “Permit checked” with the date we checked. It’s the first thing they look for and the reason they’ll pick you over a stranger on a marketplace post.

Your menu of the week

Five days, five dishes, your prices. Post it once and it shows on your page with day-by-day pricing and your pickup spot or delivery areas. Your day, week and month prices show too, plus a trial week if you offer one and whether you’re taking new customers.

Followers who get told

People follow kitchens they like. When alerts launch, every follower hears the moment you post — no group chats to maintain.

Orders the way you already take them

Your page has one big “Order on WhatsApp” button and a “Call” button. Orders and payments stay between you and your customers. We never touch them and never take a cut. Messages from Tiffin Finder start with “Hi, I found you on Tiffin Finder”, so you can see who found you here.

What your listing looks like

This is the same card every household sees in the list and on the map, built with a made-up kitchen so you can see it before yours goes up. It's clearly marked Sample listing — tap the name to see the full page it links to.

Your pickup spot

Tiffin Finder’s map shows pickup spots and delivery areas side by side — pickup and delivery matter equally here. If you offer pickup, you choose how your spot is shown: your exact address, the nearest intersection, or your neighbourhood only. Households see only what you choose, and you can change it or take it down at any time. Just message us.

If you also deliver, your delivery areas show too. If you only deliver, your listing shows your neighbourhood and delivery areas, with no pickup spot.

Copy your own link

Once your listing is live, you also get your own link. It opens your kitchen’s page on its own: your menu, prices, order button, Share button and permit line, with no other kitchens, no filters, no map and no directory menu. There’s just a small “Listed on Tiffin Finder” line at the bottom. Put it in your WhatsApp status, your Instagram bio or on a flyer.

It looks like https://tiffinfinder.ca/?k=your-kitchen&solo=1. We send it to you with your listing. To copy it again, open it and tap Share, then Copy link. See an example with a sample kitchen.

What’s free, and what “Pro” will be

Free, always: your listing, your weekly menu, your follower list and the alerts that go with it. There is no commission and no fee to be listed.

Pro, later: we plan optional paid extras for kitchens that want them — things like a featured spot in your quadrant, scheduling menus ahead, and simple stats on views and follows. None of this exists yet, there is no price yet, and nothing free will move behind Pro. We’ll tell listed kitchens first.

What “Permit checked” means

Before we list you, we ask for a copy of your current AHS Food Handling Permit and match your kitchen to its public Alberta Health Services inspection record and, where it’s visible, your City of Calgary business licence. When that checks out, your page shows “Permit checked” with the date we checked. If your permit’s expiry date passes, the badge comes off and your page shows “Permit being re-checked” until we check again.

A permit check is not an inspection, a grade, or an endorsement by Alberta Health Services, and we say so on every page. You agree to keep the permit valid, to tell us if anything changes, and to accept that we remove listings that receive an AHS closure order. The full wording is in the terms.

How to get listed

  1. 1

    Hold a valid permit

    If you don’t have one yet, start with how permits work. We can only list permitted kitchens — no exceptions, because the badge has to mean something.

  2. 2

    Send us your details

    Your kitchen’s name, quadrant and cuisine, your pickup spot (and how you want it shown) or the areas you deliver to, your WhatsApp number, and a photo or copy of your permit. Our email address is on the About page; we’ll reply within a few days.

  3. 3

    We check and publish

    We check the permit, build your page, and send you the link to approve before it goes live. You only appear with your permission.

  4. 4

    Post your weekly menu

    Send us the week’s five dishes and prices and we post them. Self-serve posting comes with a later version.
