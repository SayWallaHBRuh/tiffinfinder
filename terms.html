1. What Tiffin Finder is, and isn’t

Tiffin Finder is a directory. It lists tiffin kitchens in and around Calgary — home kitchens, restaurants, caterers and rented commercial kitchens — whose Alberta Health Services (AHS) Food Handling Permit we have checked, and shows the menu, prices and contact details each kitchen gives us.

Tiffin Finder does not take orders or payments and is not the seller of any meal. When you tap “Order on WhatsApp” or “Call”, a short panel shows you the message first. Nothing is sent until you send it from your own WhatsApp or phone, and then you are contacting the kitchen directly. Your order, payment, delivery and any refund are between you and the kitchen.

In this preview version every listing is a sample with made-up names and phone numbers. Nothing on the site is a real offer of food until real, permit-checked kitchens are added.

2. If you’re a household

  • You may browse, follow kitchens and sign up for alerts free of charge.
  • Menus, prices, pickup spots, delivery areas and availability are set by each kitchen and can change without notice. Confirm with the kitchen when you order.
  • Pickup locations are published because the kitchen chose to share them, at the precision it chose. Tiffin Finder never shows more than that. Please collect at the time you agreed with the kitchen.
  • Tell the kitchen about allergies and dietary needs before ordering. Labels such as “veg only”, “halal” or “Jain” are the kitchen’s own statements about its cooking; we display them but cannot guarantee them.
  • If you have a food-safety concern about a kitchen, contact AHS Environmental Public Health (1-833-476-4743). You can also use “Report a problem with this listing” on the kitchen’s page so we can review it.
  • Don’t misuse the site: no scraping of kitchen contact details, no harassment of kitchens, and no attempts to interfere with how the site works.

3. If you’re a kitchen

By asking to be listed, you agree that:

  • You hold a valid AHS Food Handling Permit for the kitchen where the meals are prepared, and you will keep it valid for as long as you are listed.
  • You comply with the Alberta Food Regulation and any conditions on your permit, and you hold any municipal business licence your city requires.
  • You will tell us within 48 hours if your permit is suspended, cancelled, expires, or if the kitchen you cook in changes.
  • We will remove your listing on any AHS closure order, and may remove or pause it if we cannot re-confirm your permit, if we receive credible safety complaints, or if listing details are inaccurate.
  • The information you give us — name, cuisine, menu, prices, pickup spot, delivery areas, contact details — is accurate and yours to publish, and you give us permission to display it. You can ask us to update or remove your listing at any time.
  • You choose how your pickup location is shown: exact address, nearest intersection, or neighbourhood only. We publish it only at the precision you choose and never show more. You can change or remove it at any time, and you confirm you’re entitled to share the spot you give us.
  • Listing is free. If we later offer paid “Pro” features, they will be optional and described separately before you pay for anything.
  • You’ll get your own link to your listing, which shows only your kitchen, with no other kitchens and a small “Listed on Tiffin Finder” line. You may share it anywhere. It shows the same listing, permit status and notes as the site, and you agree not to present it as an inspection or endorsement.

4. What “Permit checked” means

“Permit checked” with a date means that, on that date, we matched the kitchen the listing is for to its public Alberta Health Services inspection record and, where visible, its City of Calgary business licence. If the permit’s expiry date passes, the listing shows “Permit being re-checked” instead until we check again. “Verification pending” means we have not finished that check.

A permit check is not an inspection, a food-safety rating, or an endorsement by AHS or the City of Calgary. We do not inspect kitchens and cannot guarantee that a permitted kitchen follows every rule every day. Permits can lapse between our checks.

5. Complaints and takedowns

Every kitchen’s page has a Report a problem with this listing link. It opens a new email in your own email app, with the kitchen’s name filled in. Nothing is sent until you send it yourself. You can also email sitesbyadeel@gmail.com if a listing is inaccurate, out of date, uses your name or content without permission, or raises a safety concern. We check reports against public records only, such as the AHS inspection record and City of Calgary licence information, and we never tell the kitchen who reported it. Our target is to respond and act, by correcting, pausing or removing the listing, within 48 hours of hearing from you. Urgent food-safety matters should also go to AHS Environmental Public Health (1-833-476-4743).

6. Our content and brand

The text, design, code, logo and mascot illustration on this site are owned by Tiffin Finder. Please don’t copy, republish or reuse them — including cloning the site’s look or code for another project — without our written permission. You’re welcome to link to any page.

Kitchen names, menus and descriptions belong to the kitchens that gave them to us. Map shapes are from the City of Calgary’s and City of Airdrie’s open data, under the licences named where the map appears; those attribution lines stay wherever the data is used. You may share links to any page freely. Every kitchen’s page has a Share button that shares a link to that page.

7. Liability, in plain words

We provide the site as it is, without warranties. We are not responsible for the food, the service, delivery, pricing or conduct of any kitchen, or for anything that happens between a household and a kitchen. To the extent Alberta law allows, our liability to you for anything connected with the site is limited to nothing more than the amount you paid us — which, in this version, is zero.

8. Changes and law

We may update these terms as the service grows; the date at the top tells you when we last did. Continued use after a change means you accept it. These terms are governed by the laws of Alberta and Canada.
